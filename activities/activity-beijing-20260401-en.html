Beijing Founders Meetup | OpenClaw and the Next Stage of AI Products

Boundless Youth Forum · Beijing · 2026/04/01

Date: April 1, 2026 Location: Beijing Tags: #OpenClaw #AI #Entrepreneurship #Meetup

Event Highlights

  • 60+ founders and AI practitioners gathered in Beijing for focused discussions around OpenClaw
  • Open Mic featured 6 in-progress projects across hardware, SaaS, and multi-agent systems
  • Guest perspectives from Zhipu AI, Baidu AI Cloud, Ultrasonic Club, and Lovstudio
  • Core takeaway: building becomes easier, but strategic judgment becomes harder

Event Recap

On April 1, Boundless Youth Forum hosted a founders meetup in Beijing. More than 60 builders and entrepreneurs came together around one central question: Is OpenClaw pushing AI products into a new stage?

Open Mic: Projects in Motion

Before the main panel, founders shared what they are actively building: smart hardware for overseas use cases, a minimal-setup remote chat client, online conversion experiments for real estate and foreign-trade B2B, a multi-agent system (AgentsMesh), LOVCODE coding agents, and a global social media operations assistant.

No polished storytelling, just real progress and real bottlenecks. That made the later discussion practical rather than conceptual.

A Grounded, Frontline Discussion

Guests from Zhipu AI, Baidu AI Cloud, the founder of Ultrasonic Club, and the founder of Lovstudio shared their viewpoints from different angles: Is this a real inflection point for AI products? When can Agent deployment be called truly real? Where do AI startup opportunities actually hold?

There was no single conclusion, but each perspective was concrete and directly useful for product and go-to-market decisions.

A Clear Shared Signal

Across the afternoon, one collective signal became clear: OpenClaw is leading AI products into a new era. At the same time, participants repeatedly emphasized that while entry barriers are dropping, high-quality judgment is becoming more difficult.

Instead of waiting on the sidelines, the better strategy is to understand early, build early, and actively engage with this emerging form of digital life.

From Stage to Floor

This event had very few passive listeners. Conversations continued from Open Mic to panel, and from stage to floor throughout free exchange. Many meaningful new connections were formed in that process.

Boundless exchange, infinite future.
