Chen Zhiwei Moderates Roundtable at GOBI 2025

GOBI 2025 Global Open Source Business Innovation Conference · Beijing

Date: December 21, 2025 Location: Beijing, Haidian Tags: #Open Source #AI #GOBI #Beijing #Entrepreneurship

Event Summary

On December 21st, Boundless Youth Forum founder Chen Zhiwei was invited to attend GOBI 2025 Global Open Source Business Innovation Conference and served as moderator for Roundtable 4: "Creation · Renaissance Entrepreneurs Catalyzed by AI".

The conference was held at Renaissance Beijing Haidian Hotel, gathering over 500 open source leaders, unicorn founders, top VCs, industry executives, policymakers, and frontline developers from around the world. Online viewership exceeded 36,000, reaching the global open source and innovation community.

Organized by Upstream Labs, AI Origin Community, and CSDN, and guided by the Beijing Haidian District Talent Bureau and Zhongguancun Science City Management Committee Technology Development Department, the conference focused on the deep integration of open source, business, and AI under the theme "Unleashing Source Power, Creating the Future", exploring how open source innovation can transform into sustainable industrial value.

During the roundtable discussion, participants engaged in in-depth exchanges on the profile of new-age entrepreneurs in the AI era, interdisciplinary capability structures, and how open source ecosystems are reshaping innovation paradigms, sharing cutting-edge observations and practical experiences.

In the wave of AI, we are witnessing a new "Renaissance" — entrepreneurs are no longer just experts in a single field, but need to be polymaths with interdisciplinary integration capabilities, open source collaboration spirit, and dual vision of technology and business. Open source is not just a technical development model, but a mindset and ecosystem that is redefining the boundaries of innovation.

As a platform connecting global youth innovators, Boundless Youth Forum is committed to helping young talents find their position and achieve diverse growth in the era of open source and AI through career support, research connections, and entrepreneurship assistance. This participation in GOBI 2025 further expanded the forum's influence and resource network in the field of open source innovation.
